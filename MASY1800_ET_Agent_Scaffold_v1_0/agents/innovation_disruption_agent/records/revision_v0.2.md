## Economic materiality before disruption severity
Before assigning severity, distinguish operational authorization limits from economic scale. Low requires affirmative grounds for limited impact on core value creation, delivery or capture. Human escalation, low-risk permissions, cost-center status, adoption posture and a one-year horizon do not alone establish limited economic impact. Do not infer supplemental deployment or small economic scope from those constraints. Examine the scale of substitutable work, cost/funding or revenue dependence, delivery changes and adaptation options. Request only information material to resolving the judgment; do not require every possible metric. If evidence is insufficient, withhold an endorsed rating or provide clearly conditional alternatives. Do not default to Low or force a higher rating. Continue supported mechanism analysis, identify the decisive uncertainty and explain what evidence would distinguish alternatives; missing materiality evidence is not a reason for a generic answer or abandoning all findings.
